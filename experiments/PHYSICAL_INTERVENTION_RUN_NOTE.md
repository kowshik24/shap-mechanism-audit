# Physical source checks: clarification after opening records

The frozen protocol remains unchanged. Source validation found that all 29 light
acquisitions replay the published treatment schedule, but only 3 of 28 wind
acquisitions do so with the pinned generator/configurations. One wind file has
49 instead of the prescribed 50 measurements. The other 25 wind sequences are
retained with their failures; a replay mismatch does not prove fabrication or
invalidate the authors' physical experiments. It means the exact released
protocol-to-record association is not independently reconstructed here.

Every intervention/channel pair remains in the assessment. For an acquisition
that fails a source check, descriptive counts are saved but its two family-input
p-values are set to 1 before the fixed whole-grid Holm adjustment. No such
acquisition supplies a new empirical causal-support claim. Thus the family still
contains all 57 x 11 x 2 = 1,254 planned endpoints, rather than a smaller family
chosen by outcomes. This implements the protocol's failed-source abstention;
it does not alter a signal threshold or choose favorable acquisitions by effect.

Published graph paths and locally reconstructed randomized-response evidence
remain separate fields. Graph-only support is not independent empirical
confirmation. Missing paths, unmodeled voltage channels, non-rejections and
unreplayed experiments do not produce negatively excluded features.

Recorded clock values are not sufficiently anchored to a calendar/serial/session
identity by the retained CSVs. Report raw ranges without inferring physical
session independence from disjoint values. The fixed generator seed and distinct
measurement hashes likewise do not prove statistical independence or exchangeability.
